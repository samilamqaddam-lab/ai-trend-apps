🧠 How AutoResearch Works

  • Start with a domain-specific research prompt
  • Single-GPU training, ~630 lines of Python
  • AI generates hypotheses and explores literature
  • Human feedback shapes the next training run
  • Each loop specializes the model further
  • Output: a fine-tuned expert for your domain

💡 Why This Matters

  • Own your model — no vendor lock-in
  • Specialists beat generalists on niche tasks
  • Private data stays private
  • Control over model updates and direction
  • Cost-efficient inference once trained
  • Foundation for autonomous research agents
