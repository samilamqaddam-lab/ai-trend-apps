# AI Caricature Prompt Generator

**Date:** February 11, 2026  
**Trend:** ChatGPT Caricature Trend  
**Status:** 🔥 Viral on Twitter/X

## The Trend

In early February 2026, a viral trend exploded on social media where people use ChatGPT and other AI image generators to create exaggerated cartoon caricatures of themselves. The twist? These caricatures are based on their work, hobbies, and personality traits, all squished into one expressive, fun cartoon character.

The trend started gaining massive traction with thousands of people sharing their AI-generated caricatures, each one uniquely capturing their professional and personal essence in an entertaining, exaggerated way.

### Why It Went Viral:
- **Personal yet playful** - Everyone wants to see themselves as a cartoon
- **Highly shareable** - Visual content spreads fast on social media
- **Easy to participate** - Just describe yourself to an AI
- **Creative results** - Each caricature is unique and often hilariously accurate

## The App

This web app helps users craft the **perfect caricature prompt** to use with ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI image generator. Instead of struggling with what to write, users simply:

1. **Fill in their details**: Profession, hobbies, personality traits, and favorite objects
2. **Choose a style**: Cartoon, Pixar, Comic, Vintage, Anime, or Realistic
3. **Generate a prompt**: The app creates a detailed, optimized prompt
4. **Copy & paste**: Take the prompt to any AI image generator

### Features:
- ✨ **Smart prompt generation** - Creates detailed, effective prompts
- 🎨 **6 art styles** - From cartoon to anime to vintage poster
- 📋 **One-click copy** - Instant clipboard integration
- 💡 **Example gallery** - See what's possible
- 📱 **Fully responsive** - Works on all devices
- 🚀 **No API needed** - Works completely offline

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in any web browser
2. Fill in your information:
   - Your profession/work
   - Your hobbies (comma-separated)
   - Your personality traits (comma-separated)
   - Optional: Favorite tools or objects
3. Select your preferred art style
4. Click "Generate My Caricature Prompt"
5. Copy the generated prompt
6. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, DALL-E, Midjourney, or any AI image generator

## Example Prompts

**Software Developer:**
> "Create a vibrant cartoon caricature of a Software Developer. Their personality is Creative, Problem-solver, Night owl, which should be reflected in their expression and pose. Surround them with exaggerated, floating elements representing their hobbies: Coding, Gaming, Coffee brewing. Include these signature items: Laptop, Mechanical keyboard, Energy drink. The caricature should have exaggerated features that capture their essence - make their professional and personal characteristics visually prominent and fun."

**Fitness Trainer:**
> "Create a Pixar-style 3D animated character of a Fitness Trainer. Their personality is Energetic, Motivating, Disciplined, which should be reflected in their expression and pose. Surround them with exaggerated, floating elements representing their hobbies: Weightlifting, Yoga, Meal prep. Include these signature items: Dumbbell, Protein shaker, Workout timer."
