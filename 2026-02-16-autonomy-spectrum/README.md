## The 5 Levels

1. **Pure Copilot (0-20%)** — AI suggests, human executes everything (GitHub Copilot, Grammarly)
2. **Smart Assistant (21-40%)** — AI does simple tasks with oversight (Microsoft Copilot, Notion AI)
3. **Hybrid Copilot (41-60%)** — AI handles workflows, checks in at key points (Cursor IDE, Claude Code)
4. **Semi-Autonomous Agent (61-80%)** — AI completes tasks end-to-end, reports results (Browser Use, Devon AI)
5. **Fully Autonomous Agent (81-100%)** — AI operates independently 24/7 (Palantir AIP, trading bots)
